How CropLink Works

From fertile fields directly to your kitchen in three transparent steps.

1. Farmers Harvest

Farmers list their crops with harvest dates, grade certifications, and transparent direct pricing.

2. You Order Direct

Customers browse verified local farm crops, choose delivery modes, and order directly without broker margins.

3. Fast Doorstep Delivery

Partner delivery agents swiftly pick up packed goods from farms and drop them right to your doorstep within hours.
